07 · Audience-specific scripts
Example
video hooks

Grab the hook that fits your niche. Adapt the wording freely — the angle is what matters.

Nurses
"Nurses don't get enough credit for the gear that actually saves lives. This is mine."
Taps into Nurses Week pride. Works perfectly timed around May 6 & 12. High emotional resonance.
"I've been a nurse for [X] years. I wish I'd had this on day one."
Retrospective format — great for experienced nurses sharing wisdom. Builds credibility instantly.
EMT / Paramedic
"POV: You're first on scene and seconds matter. Here's what's always on my belt."
POV format is high-performing on Reels/TikTok. Creates urgency from the first frame.
"What's actually in a paramedic's kit? Let me show you the thing most people overlook."
"What's in my bag" is evergreen for this audience — drives high engagement and repeat views.
Firefighters / Police
"Every second counts on scene. This is the one tool I never leave the truck without."
Urgency-led hook works well for both firefighter and police audiences. Emphasizes duty-grade reliability.
"6 tools. 1 clip. The gear that goes everywhere I do on shift."
EDC-angle hook appeals to the tactical/gear community. Works as a Reel overlay or talking head.
